Zelda on PC runs your own copy of The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild (Wii U version) on the Cemu emulator, set up as a PC game: keyboard and mouse controls with on-screen key prompts, a PC settings menu, mouse support in the game's menus and AMD FSR 3 frame generation.

NO GAME FILES ARE INCLUDED. You need your own dump of the game, as folders that contain "code", "content" and "meta":

  - the base game (Europe, Americas or Japan)
  - the update 1.5.0 (required)
  - the DLC (optional)

The next steps ask where to install Zelda on PC and where your game dump is. Nothing is copied from or changed in your game folder.

Zelda on PC is an unofficial fan project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo or the Cemu project.
